## Design and Embroidery Notes

This is an adaptation of the
[Ink/Stitch hat file](/tutorials/resources/inkstitch-logo/). A tutorial to sew
the cap is available from
[Evan Roth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NEMFzVAeBDs). The needle part of
the logo has been left as is because a minimum width of 3mm is needed for the
foam to cut cleanly.  The text "INK" and "STITCH" have been modified.  The
stitch density has been increased and pull compensation of "0.5mm" added to
allow cutting and covering of embroidery foam to give a raised effect.  The
letters in "STITCH" have each been slightly reduced in size to ensure they do
not touch each other.  Caps have been added to cut the foam in regions where
the stitch direction is parallel to the edge of the foam.  Planks have been
added to keep the foam together where two regions join but do not have stitches
across the full region.

![Embroidery preview](/assets/images/tutorials/inkstitch-logo-3d/inkstitch-3d-preview.png)

It is helpful to use an 80/12 needle for the text "INK" and "STITCH", a regular
75/11 needle should be used for the needle part of the logo. Before
embroidering, iron interfacing onto the back of the cloth used for the front
part of the cap to give it more structure so that the foam is held up once the
embroidery is done and the cap has been sewn together.  Then add your
stabilizer and hoop the cloth.  Embroider the needle part of the logo. Stop the
embroidery machine and place the embroidery foam inside the hoop.  Finish
embroidering, remove the hoop from the machine and tear away the embroidery
foam.

![Embroidering on top of foam](/assets/images/tutorials/inkstitch-logo-3d/inkstitch-3d-embroidering.jpg)

Once embroidering is complete, sew the cap together.

Further example DST embroidery files can be found at
[https://victordigitizing.net/free-files](https://victordigitizing.net/free-files).
Further tips on 3D embroidery can be found at
[https://www.mrxstitch.com/3d-foam-machine-embroidery](https://www.mrxstitch.com/3d-foam-machine-embroidery).
